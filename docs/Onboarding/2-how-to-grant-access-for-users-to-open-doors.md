Here's how to grant users access to open Doors, step by step:

* [Directory Locks Setup](docs:assign-locks-to-directories): Associate physical Latch Cortex Locks with OpenDoor Directory Items of ENTRANCE type.
* [Access Paths Setup](docs:access-paths-setup): Create Access Paths that define routes between Doors in the building.
* [Permission Sets Setup](docs:permission-stes-setup): Define Sets of Permissions that define capabilities Roles will have.
* [Roles Setup](docs:roles-setup): Specify the different Role Types in the building, what actions can they do (based on Permission Sets) and where they are applicable (Scopes).
* [Role Assignments](docs:role-assignments): Assign a Role to a User to specify what access it has and where.

After these steps are completed the User can see and open the Doors it has access to in the Door/SDK mobile apps.
